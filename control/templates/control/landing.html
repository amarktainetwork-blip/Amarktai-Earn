WHAT IS IT, REALLY?

An AI team with a business brain.

Most AI tools wait for you to give them a task. AmarktAI Earn is designed to do more: watch approved earning channels, compare opportunities, organise the work, execute it through the right workers and track what actually gets paid.

It is not meant to chase everything. A bad job can waste time, credits and reputation. Earn is designed to say yes to the right work and no to the wrong work.

PROFIT, SAFETY & SETTLEMENT

Automation that knows
when to stop.

Earn is built around permission and evidence. The Profit Brain evaluates expected economics before resources are committed. GenX supplies compatible AI capabilities when required, while safety gates keep account, payout, policy and quality proof separate.

01

Profit Brain

Compares reward, marketplace fees, GenX credits, other expected cost, time and risk. Expected profit stays a forecast—not revenue.

02

GenX execution

Selects compatible provider capabilities for the task at runtime, records actual calls and stops ambiguous remote work for reconciliation.

03

Approval controls

Accounts, automation policy, acquisition, publishing and payout proof each keep their own fail-closed gate under owner control.

04

Settlement truth

Earned, pending and settled remain distinct. The console calls money received only after authoritative payout and reconciliation evidence exists.
